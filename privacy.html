Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Xpenzes ("we", "us") helps you track and split shared expenses with friends. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address, name, username, and password (stored securely by our authentication provider). If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google.
  • Content you create: groups, group members, expenses, how expenses are split, and payments you record as settled.
  • Social connections: friend requests and the friends you add, so they can find you by username and share groups with you.
  • Notifications: records of friend requests, group invites and payment activity shown in the app.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or payment card or bank details. Xpenzes records payments between friends but does not process or move money.

How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To show your groups, expenses and balances, and share them with the members of those groups.
  • To let other users find you by username and send you friend requests.
  • To provide support when you contact us.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information.
  • We don't show ads or use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Who can see your information

Your username and name are visible to other Xpenzes users who search for you or share a group with you. Expenses and balances in a group are visible to members of that group.

Data is stored with Supabase, which hosts our database and authentication. If you use Google sign-in, Google's privacy policy also applies to that sign-in.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and its associated data at any time through our support page. Expenses you shared in groups may remain visible to other group members where needed to keep their balances accurate, but will no longer be linked to your account.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and access to your data is restricted so that only you and the members of your groups can see it.

Children

Xpenzes is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us through the Xpenzes support page.
