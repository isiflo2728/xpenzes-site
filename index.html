Frequently asked questions

How do I split an expense?

Open a group, tap Add Expense, enter the amount, and choose to split it equally or enter custom amounts for each member.

How do I settle up?

Open the group and check Group Balances to see who owes whom. Use Settle Up to record a payment once it's been made. Xpenzes records payments; it does not move money.

How do I add friends?

Go to Friends, tap Add Friend, and search for their username. They'll get a request in their notifications.

How do I delete my account?

Open a support request with the email address on your account and we'll delete your account and its data.
